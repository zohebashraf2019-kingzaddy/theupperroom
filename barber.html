Meet the barber

From Bgotskillz
to The Upper Room

Brian Puente has spent nine years behind the chair — a Nevada native who built his name on talent, consistency and hard work. What started as Bgotskillz has grown into something with a greater purpose: The Upper Room Barber Studio.

From clean classic cuts, fades and tapers to modern styles and detailed hair designs, barbering is more than cutting hair. It’s art, creativity and the freedom to express yourself without being afraid of making mistakes.

“If you make a mistake, don’t quit. Learn from it, pick your cross back up, and keep going. Growth comes from showing up again and again.”

Brian Puente
