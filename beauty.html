A new name · A new season

From The Pink Room to The Upper Room

After six years of growing, learning and pouring her heart into her craft, Evelyn Puente’s studio stepped into a new chapter. What many of you knew as The Pink Room is now The Upper Room Beauty Studio — a name that represents growth, surrender, faith, and trusting God’s timing.

Evelyn is a licensed esthetician and lash artist specializing in custom lash extensions and Korean lash lifts. Soft and natural, textured and wispy, or full-volume glam — every look is customized to the person on her lash bed.

And this chapter is a family one. Alongside the beauty studio, her husband Brian brings nine years of barbering to The Upper Room Barber Studio, right next door. One roof, two crafts, and faith at the center of it all.

Before you book

Booking & cancellation policy

Card on file

A card on file is required at the time of booking for no-show protection. Some services require a deposit.

Within 48 hours

50% of the total service cost is charged for cancellations within 48 hours of your appointment.

Within 24 hours / no-show

100% of the service cost is charged for no-shows and last-minute cancellations. Payment is needed before rescheduling.
